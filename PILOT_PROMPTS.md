# Pilot Prompt Pack

## Pilot Format
- Target length: about 60 seconds
- Visual style: cinematic historical documentary
- Aspect: 16:9 landscape
- Tone: premium, realistic, restrained, no fantasy styling

## Shared Continuity Rules
- Historical Phoenician setting in the central Mediterranean
- Warm limestone, cedar wood, linen sails, amphorae, volcanic millstones
- Natural sunlight, realistic sea conditions, documentary realism
- No modern buildings unless the shot is explicitly modern
- No text inside the generated image
- No fantasy armor, no Roman imperial motifs, no anachronistic weapons

## Scene 1
- Duration target: 12-15 seconds
- Purpose: establish trade network and scale
- Prompt:
  `Use case: historical-scene
  Asset type: video pilot keyframe
  Primary request: a cinematic wide shot of a fleet of Phoenician merchant ships crossing the calm Mediterranean at dawn
  Scene/backdrop: open sea with soft golden sunrise and distant haze
  Subject: multiple Phoenician merchant ships with rounded hulls, square linen sails, cedar wood construction
  Style/medium: photoreal historical documentary frame
  Composition/framing: very wide establishing shot, low camera near waterline, lead ship centered with supporting ships staggered behind
  Lighting/mood: dawn light, calm, majestic, strategic maritime power
  Color palette: gold, sea blue, cedar brown, pale linen
  Materials/textures: weathered wood, linen sails, rippling water
  Constraints: historically grounded, realistic sail rigging, no text, no watermark
  Avoid: fantasy ships, Roman ships, stormy sea, modern objects`

## Scene 2
- Duration target: 12-15 seconds
- Purpose: show cargo and active trade
- Prompt:
  `Use case: historical-scene
  Asset type: video pilot keyframe
  Primary request: Phoenician merchants loading trade goods onto a ship at a busy stone quay
  Scene/backdrop: ancient Mediterranean harbor quay in Malta with limestone dock and moored merchant vessel
  Subject: merchants and dock workers moving decorated amphorae and volcanic stone millstones into a cargo ship
  Style/medium: photoreal historical documentary frame
  Composition/framing: medium-wide shot from quay level, visible cargo in foreground and ship hull in background
  Lighting/mood: warm morning sunlight, industrious and prosperous
  Color palette: warm limestone, terracotta, muted textiles, blue-green water
  Materials/textures: carved stone, rope, pottery glaze, coarse fabric, wood grain
  Constraints: authentic period clothing, visible trade goods, no text, no watermark
  Avoid: cranes, metal containers, modern port equipment, fantasy costumes`

## Scene 3
- Duration target: 12-15 seconds
- Purpose: establish Bormla and the harbor advantage
- Prompt:
  `Use case: historical-scene
  Asset type: video pilot keyframe
  Primary request: an elevated cinematic view of the ancient Grand Harbour and Bormla as a sheltered strategic hub
  Scene/backdrop: limestone inlets, protected harbor waters, natural elevations around the port, ancient era Malta
  Subject: sheltered harbor with Phoenician vessels anchored safely near the shore
  Style/medium: photoreal historical documentary frame
  Composition/framing: high wide angle showing the geography of the harbor and the calm protected water
  Lighting/mood: late afternoon Mediterranean sun, secure, strategic, expansive
  Color palette: honey limestone, turquoise water, dusty earth, soft sky
  Materials/textures: carved shoreline, sailcloth, wood hulls, calm reflective water
  Constraints: focus on natural harbor protection and strategic geography, no modern skyline, no text
  Avoid: modern fortifications, cruise ships, fantasy city design`

## Scene 4
- Duration target: 12-15 seconds
- Purpose: end on engineering and craft
- Prompt:
  `Use case: historical-scene
  Asset type: video pilot keyframe
  Primary request: ancient shipwrights building or repairing a Phoenician Gaulos ship using mortise and tenon craftsmanship
  Scene/backdrop: shaded limestone inlet or open shipyard near Bormla with timber and tools
  Subject: craftsmen fitting cedar planks with hardwood pegs, visible curved hull of a Gaulos ship under construction or repair
  Style/medium: photoreal historical documentary frame
  Composition/framing: close-medium cinematic shot focused on hands, hull structure, and joinery detail
  Lighting/mood: warm directional sunlight with floating dust, serious technical craftsmanship
  Color palette: cedar brown, limestone beige, natural resin black, linen off-white
  Materials/textures: wood fibers, resin, rope, hand tools, weathered skin, stone dust
  Constraints: historically grounded tools and construction methods, no text, no watermark
  Avoid: steel nails, power tools, modern workshop elements, fantasy styling`
