# Pilot Video Prompts

Use these with an image-to-video model, starting from the existing pilot keyframes in `pilot/assets/`.

## Global Rules
- Duration: 5 to 7 seconds per shot
- Camera motion: slow and cinematic
- Preserve composition and historical accuracy
- Documentary realism, not fantasy
- No morphing faces, extra limbs, or changing architecture
- No modern elements

## Scene 1: Fleet at Sea
Source image: `pilot/assets/scene01_fleet.png`

Prompt:
`Animate this historical Phoenician fleet scene with subtle realistic motion. The lead ship moves slowly forward through calm Mediterranean water, the hull rises gently with the swell, the sails flex naturally in a light wind, and a soft wake forms around the bow. Keep the sunrise lighting warm and stable. Preserve the original composition, historical accuracy, and cinematic documentary tone. No modern elements, no fantasy styling, no distortion.`

## Scene 2: Trade Quay
Source image: `pilot/assets/scene02_trade.png`
